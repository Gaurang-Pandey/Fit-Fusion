Welcome to **Fit-Fusion** – Your Ultimate Fitness Companion!

---

## Overview

**Fit-Fusion** is a dynamic web application designed to empower you on your health journey. Whether you’re curious about your BMI, need guidance on your caloric needs, or want personalized exercise recommendations, Fit-Fusion has got you covered. Our sleek, modern interface and interactive tools make tracking your health fun and motivating.

---

## Features

- **BMI Calculator:** Quickly calculate your Body Mass Index to understand your health baseline.
- **Caloric Needs Guide:** Receive personalized insights on your daily caloric requirements.
- **Exercise Recommendations:** Access tailored exercise routines to match your fitness goals.
- **Modern, Responsive Design:** Enjoy a user-friendly experience built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Bootstrap.
- **Motivational Content:** Get inspired by motivational quotes from fitness icons.

## Getting Started

### Prerequisites

- A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, etc.)
- *(Optional)* A local server for development (e.g., [Live Server](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=ritwickdey.LiveServer) for VSCode)

### Installation

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Gaurang-Pandey/Fit-Fusion.git
2. **Navigate to the project directory:**
   ```bash
   cd Fit-Fusion
3. **Open the application:**
   Open index.html in your browser directly, or run it on a local server.

## Usage

• Use the navigation bar to switch between tools: BMI Calculator, Caloric Needs, Exercise Guide, and more.

• Input your details in the BMI Calculator to receive instant feedback.

• Explore exercise recommendations and caloric insights to plan your fitness routine.
